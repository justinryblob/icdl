The truth about atoms is that nobody really understands them.

Well, not entirely. Scientists nowadays have formulas and models that can predict an atom’s behaviour with remarkable accuracy. What we don't have is a simple, intuitive way of imagining what an atom actually is. Our everyday intuition begins to break down when we shrink ourselves down to the atomic scale, and many of the “rules” you've been taught are really simplified models of something much stranger underneath.

Atoms are just weird like that. Yet they’re also some of the most beautifully structured systems we have ever discovered in nature. By improving our understanding of the architecture of atoms, we can start to make some sense of their strange nature, and eventually understand why they do… all of that.
